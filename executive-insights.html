Fiscal Settings

Configure your fiscal year start month and key financial data to ensure your Executive Insights reflect your organization's real performance. Completing these settings allows you to unlock accurate ROI tracking, KPI monitoring, and empowering better strategic decisions.

Estimated Financial Budget * info
Enter your organization's forward-looking monthly budget from a company-wide perspective. Edits here won't affect your General Budget. Currency in USD.
Expected ROI * info
Enter the expected return on investment for each month. This is how much value you expect to realize for every $1 spent on cloud.
Cloud Spend as a % of Revenue
Track cloud cost as a percentage of company revenue. Both fields below are optional; leave blank to hide this section on the dashboard.
